Visa & work-authorization signals. This tab reads every hospitality & food-service postings — kitchens, service, hotels and food craft in the loaded dataset and detects, per posting, whether the employer states any visa, residence-permit, work-permit, EU/EEA-status, Blue Card, qualification-recognition (Anerkennung) or relocation-support signal. The same rule engine and context vetoes as GATI's extraction pipeline are used, so false positives such as "Visa credit card" or equal-opportunity boilerplate are filtered out. Figures are shown for the ten largest ISCO-4 occupations as a share of that occupation's postings.
